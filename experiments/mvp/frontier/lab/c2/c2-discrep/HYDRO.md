# Checker-derived linear physics

`hydro.py:HydroFeatures` reads the actual vessel, boxes and cells, and copies no coefficients from cargo literature. Its `vector(pattern)` uses tonnes for mass and tonne metres for moments, and returns mass, longitudinal moment, transverse moment, vertical moment, raw cargo midship bending contribution in N m, then per-bay mass. Long-box mass is divided equally over supporting cells, matching the checker strength point-load convention. All three mass moments use averaged physical cell coordinates and cumulative **actual** box heights plus half the current box height, matching `checker/stability_loading.py:loading_condition`; the inherited `pages.height_of` approximation is not used. `feature_scales` gives vessel/cargo dimensional scales for normalization; these are not legal bounds.

`rows()` returns `LinearRow(name, coefficients, lower, upper, note)` in these same feature coordinates. Independent displacement bounds come from `_loading_policy`, including any valid policy tightening. Draft is a conservative even-keel mass proxy at the lesser of hull/berth draft. Trim boundary rows solve the checker's submerged volume at each legal signed trim and known full-load displacement; projecting the resulting buoyancy and mass moments onto its longitudinal waterplane axis gives a linear plane involving longitudinal and vertical moment. Heel boundary rows do the same at the declared signed list limits, using its transverse axis. This avoids guessing the trim sign or a tonnes-per-centimetre coefficient. GM has an even-keel upper vertical-moment row derived from `full_submerged` KB+BM, the checker's minimum GM, and the same fixed-tank free-surface correction.

These rows are **surrogates**: trim planes hold heel at zero, heel planes hold trim at zero; liquid CG is fixed upright; GM is the even-keel tangent criterion. The full checker solves coupled attitudes, physical liquid shifts, and GZ/containership second checks. PhysicalModel's rows are soft; they are construction guidance, not an independent safety certificate. No legal bending or bay capacity is invented: those unbounded rows exist to preserve LP functionals in rounding. The cargo midship bending proxy omits buoyancy, fixed loads and the checker's affine closure correction. The visibility/lashing equilibrium treats asymmetric OOG centres differently from the stability mass accumulator; the linear moments deliberately match stability, so they do not encode that OOG-specific alternate centre.

`checker_margins(report)` extracts signed legal-positive values from the full checker's own `PartReport.margins`, minimized over all reported voyage and intermediate states. Distinct units remain separate: securing and bending ratios, trim/draft/GM/sight metres, heel/sight radians, displacement kg, and containership second-check area / lever / angle margins. Unavailable parts and absent margins are explicit; `None` never means a pass. Every individual keyed margin is retained under `all`. Complete replay-legal stows and full-checker passes must still be counted separately.
