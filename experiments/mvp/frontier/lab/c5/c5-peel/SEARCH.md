Search implementation and frozen smoke evidence

The D242 revision below supersedes the early four-second work limits and earlier smoke descriptions in this development history.

The search uses a private subclass of the snapshot page model; no live engine import or checker mutation is needed. Its restricted CP-SAT master has a slack variable per load-list box. It minimises boxes ashore, then TEU ashore, then gross kilograms ashore, fixing each proven minimum before moving to the next objective. Later objectives minimise the per-column soft securing/stack breach proxy and the existing page cost. Full-checker rules and measured breaches, rather than these construction proxies, rank finished plans in the controller.

L4 securing uses excess ratio as a cost and tightens the permitted upper ratio through infinity, 1.3 and 1.05 across three cold page models. L5 also penalises stack-weight excess in tonnes. Tier/height fit and the page model's stronger IMDG/OOG guards stay enforced. Whole-vessel nonlinear physics is not represented exactly in the page master: the controller's bounded ballast recipes and unchanged full checker compare those breaches. A candidate is not a safety pass merely because a page model accepted it. This finite search makes no feasibility or minimum-breach proof.

Fixed construction limits are three page models per level, 8 phase-one and 4 phase-three pricing rounds per model, 10,000 CP conflicts and 0.5 deterministic seconds per lexicographic objective, and 500 heel swaps. The first model receives 90% of the remaining allowance; each integer master always gets a finite one-second lane, even after an atomic pricing overrun. A greedy feasible subset is also retained and used as a CP-SAT hint, so presolve timeout cannot erase all partial candidates. Partial-mass coefficients are rounded to grams. Wall time is an additional stop. Atomic pricing/replay/checker work can overrun the wall stop. HiGHS receives threads=1 explicitly through a temporary wrapper restored in finally; SciPy reports that it passes this option to HiGHS verbatim.

Five focused tests passed in the parent's combined suite: the restricted master chooses count, TEU and mass in order; impossible complete coverage is refused; a real three-of-four tiny plan replays legally and the unchanged checker lists QZXU0000035 in objective.all_loaded; HiGHS wrapper restoration; soft L4 securing and L5 weight preserve the T0 height guard.

Cold smoke: random.tiny.20000040.0.2, seed 0, L3, four seconds of requested search, under the process-local L3 masks. Search returned two distinct replay-legal orders at 7 lifts, loading all four boxes. Independently running the unchanged checker on each reported exactly one hard rule, expert.e8.away_from_heat, with no other hard violation. The implementation therefore expresses the explicitly permitted alternative of breaking only the heat booking rather than leaving QZXU0000035 ashore. Exact rows are in search-smoke.json. This tiny map is an explicit contract test, separate from the six medium development maps.

Limitations: only empty-arrival page families are generated; onboard/discharge cases fail closed. CP-SAT's restricted column family and deterministic work limit do not establish global optimality. Current page pricing does not encode every T1 or nonlinear whole-vessel physics relaxation; L5 retains several stronger guards voluntarily. Partial OOG repair temporarily restricts only the local model box list and restores it in finally. No stored plan, diagnostic archive, warm reference, or prior result is read at runtime.

Development correction before final freeze: the first D185 map initially produced no candidates because three short pricing shares left no integer work. After allocating most time to the first model and adding a retained greedy subset and feasible CP hints, generator-only L3 smoke (seed 0, requested 4 seconds) returned a 106-of-121-box candidate, 61 lifts, 15 ashore / 27 TEU / 366189.6176252655 kg, in 2.998 seconds. These are construction diagnostics, not full-checker acceptance claims. Follow-up pricing receives a 0.12 second minimum so it can create columns rather than returning only the initial all-uncovered LP.

D242 completion revision, before refreeze

The ladder now seeks complete stows first. generate(..., context={'complete_only': True}) creates no ashore variables in its integer master and never returns a partial greedy fallback. Complete-only defaults true at L4 and L5. Complete candidates are retained even if later, stricter construction guidance cannot reconstruct them; the unchanged full checker and controller decide their admissible tier. Default L3 retains complete booking-breached candidates and hard-securing alternatives that can be partial. Ashore count, TEU and mass precede rule count, breach and lifts. A partial plan is never a completed map.

Final finite work caps: three page models per level; 40 phase-one and 20 phase-three root pricing rounds per model; complete diving with at most 80 generate calls, five pricing rounds per node, four retreats and a 12-box tail; 1,000,000 CP conflicts and 30 deterministic seconds per lexicographic objective; each master receives at least a ten-second wall lane; each tail master is capped at ten seconds; 500 heel swaps. A 120-second proposal allowance is divided 45% to the first model and then equally among remaining models. Atomic pricing, expansion and the minimum master lane may overrun that proposal stop. Every CP solve uses one worker and HiGHS receives threads=1 explicitly. The private page subclass now uses the snapshot's pages_fast implementation.

Within one invocation only, the prior chosen selection and at most 4,000 other prior page columns are copied into a new model after every current column gate. A complete prior selection, when valid, is reused as an integer hint. context['complete_seeds'] accepts an in-memory list of Scored objects, MovePlans or action tuples from the same invocation's ordinary engine lane. Seeds are replayed, required complete and converted through current page gates. Paths and saved serialized result dictionaries are rejected. No saved result or plan is loaded at runtime.

The initial strengthened D185 complete-only smoke exhausted three fixed pattern pools in 16.30 seconds without a complete candidate despite LP uncovered zero. This prompted the bounded complete dive: remaining-box pricing builds new tail columns rather than asking the same full-set pool again. Later tighter models returned UNKNOWN and did not erase the first complete candidate. This smoke did not run the full checker; it proves construction/replay completeness only. Exact metadata is in complete-search-smoke.json. Only the previously fixed first development map was used.
