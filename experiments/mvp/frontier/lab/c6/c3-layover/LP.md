## True arrival targets and loading-only tank seed

`targets.py` calls the native visibility/loading equilibrium on the original input ship. It subtracts the actual original decision-cargo mass and physical moments, using the same support geometry and OOG CG offsets as that equilibrium. The remainder includes lightship, non-decision fixed cargo, fixed loads and the inherited finite-angle liquid moments. Modeled departure cargo (including retained prefix cargo, excluding due cargo) is then added exactly once. Native submerged hull geometry at the input port density is inverted with 32 bisections to estimate the loaded even-keel LCB and KM. Target total TCG is zero; the vertical target is displacement times `(KM - true-arrival FSC - reference GM)`. The true-arrival FSC is recovered from native `KM-KG-GM`, rather than replaced by an arbitrary constant. The initial motion uses the actual arrival GM, mean draught and block coefficient. Parent outer-loop guidance may update GM, longitudinal shift and vertical restriction.

These targets remain heuristic: departure tank centroids, free surfaces and KM change with cargo and ballast; an even-keel moment target is not a physical certificate. The native full checker evaluates the final plan and all voyage conditions. No dense-medium `.48*LPP` assumption enters this target.

`loading_seed.py` copies only `loading_seed` from C4 `ballast_local.py`, importing the local frozen copy of C4 `ballast_loading.py`. It builds one affine loading LP over native ExactContext states for all three voyage phases. Added optional `search_gm_floor` is an inward search restriction; it cannot establish infeasibility. No area or securing oracle is called by this seed. The seed changes adjustable tanks only and needs subsequent full checking; local half-fill tank arms/FSM and hull tangents may miss a feasible recipe.

## Integer-tail soft-row correction (development V2)

The first development runs exposed an inherited C5 conversion error: CP-SAT rounded `-scale*.001` upward to zero when a large physical moment coefficient made `scale < 1000`. This silently converted a soft moment target into a hard constraint. The old objective also rounded `.1*moment_weight` to zero, losing the intended moment price.

The corrected tail chooses a physical quantum separately for each shared slack. Across all rows using that slack it is at least `.001` and at least `2/(scale*abs(coefficient))`. Thus every rounded negative slack coefficient remains at most -1. Physical box coefficients are still rounded upward and right-hand sides downward, preserving conservative hard-row conversion. Slack upper bounds come from the maximum possible integer row excess; they cannot accidentally harden the soft target. Constant soft rows, including unavoidable fixed-prefix contributions, are included and priced. A common objective scale of 10,000 applies to both box costs and physical slack costs. Quantization can be coarse, so no claim of exact real-valued LP objective equivalence is made. The corrected model preserves the soft-row meaning and the physical cost units; full checking still decides legality.

The dive no longer repeats an identical full integer tail after fixing history is exhausted. New neighborhoods can still be attempted by releasing the most recent fixing batch.

V2 verification: all 24 LP/row/target tests passed (PID 8087, exit 0; `lp-tests-v2.log`). Regression cases require an unreachable soft longitudinal target to admit a complete integer point, preserve the price of an unavoidable constant soft target, reject hard stack overload despite soft moments, and avoid identical end-game retries. ## Joint cargo/ballast assignment (development joint V1)

`Settings.joint_loading` accepts a local builder callable, evaluated after the box-to-slot edge list is known. Its row block supplies `A_pair`, `A_aux`, `b`, `bounds`, `quantum`, `cost` and diagnostic names. Physical auxiliary columns follow all original assignment/uncovered/soft variables. Tank fractions and three-state aft/forward draughts have explicit finite bounds and remain ordinary physical variables, never violation slacks. LP solve and all fixed-prefix bounds apply to the combined matrix. `last_joint_solution` holds the most recent successful auxiliary vector for recipe decoding.

The local assembler optionally prepends cargo-edge columns (`cargo_mass`, `cargo_moments`) and subtracts the reference plan's exact decision-cargo mass/moments from each phase's fixed base (`removed_cargo`). All retained arrival boxes remain exact fixed assignment edges, so their securing, support and mass contributions stay present once. Background cargo, lightship and fixed liquids are not subtracted. Cargo coordinates use the constructor's full physical tiers, with exact fixed-prefix heights. No checker rule changes.

**Joint integer tail defaults to SciPy/HiGHS MILP:** box edges are binary, physical ballast/draught variables and existing priced soft slacks are continuous. Every box must be assigned; uncovered variables are fixed to zero. All original hard rows, exact affine hydrostatic equalities and auxiliary-only constraints are retained. HiGHS uses one thread and a finite `mip_nodes` limit (default 2,000), with no wall deadline. A node-limited incumbent is used only after checking the exact row residuals, box integrality and fixed-prefix map. It is a candidate for ordinary full checking, not proof about the nonlinear ship or the map.

A generic CP-SAT physical-auxiliary tail remains optional (`joint_tail_solver='cp'`), using declared tank/draught grids, conservative integer coefficient conversion and separate shared-soft quantization. Exact hydrostatic equalities can be unattainable on a finite grid; optional `auxiliary_quantum` in the assembler therefore declares a construction-only equilibrium band equal to one full auxiliary grid step in each physical mass/moment row. It does not alter draft, GM, mass, list or checker limits. The default continuous MILP does **not** use this band.

Verification: all 33 LP/row/target/loading/joint tests passed (PID 10510, exit 0; `results/lp-tests-joint-v1.log`). Tests cover retained prefixes with movable cargo and bounded water/draught, auxiliary-only infeasibility in both tails, an exact 1/3 tank fraction unavailable on the CP grid but feasible in MILP, and algebraic equivalence between a reference fixed-cargo assembler and the new subtract-and-reinsert cargo columns. ## Anchored balances, native CB and pressed-or-empty lane (code added after joint V1)

Mass and longitudinal equilibrium rows accept native reference anchors: `buoyancy_kg = reference_total_affine_mass - native_reference_buoyancy` and `longitudinal_kgm = reference_affine_longitudinal_moment - native_reference_buoyancy_moment`, added once to their physical RHS. This places the same-invocation reference cargo/tank fractions exactly on the local hull balance before changing assignments. It compensates local reference heel/tank-arm differences, without modifying any physical policy bound. The arrival builder computes these anchors; the assembler records them. Target motion CB now matches native securing's **scantling** hull-volume definition; arrival draught and GM remain actual-state values.

The experimental joint MILP forwards native HiGHS `mip_max_improving_sols` (default one) and `simplex_iteration_limit` (default 20,000), alongside its node limit. **Correction from installed HiGHS 1.12 option documentation: `simplex_iteration_limit` applies when solving standalone LPs, but explicitly does NOT limit subproblems inside the MIP solver.** The 20,000 value therefore provides no bound on MIP root/node LP work. Node and improving-solution counts also do not bound root cut generation, root heuristics or presolve work. A medium development tail with about 37,800 edges consequently exceeded six minutes before returning. This is the reason the primary method switches to a bounded CP tail; no speed or strict-work claim is made for the retained experimental MILP lane.

Installed SciPy source explicitly forwards native options and retains solution-limit incumbents. Returned incumbents are checked for finite values, all variable bounds, all hard rows, cargo equations and every integral variable. Native option introspection confirmed that `mip_max_cut_passes` and `mip_lp_iteration_limit` are unavailable. Heuristic flags/effort and symmetry detection can be disabled, but would still leave MIP subproblem work without a global iteration cap. A callback wrapper was considered and not implemented.

An optional pressed-or-empty lane adds `aux_integrality` to the joint row specification. Fraction variables with flag one must be actual integer fractions 0 or 1 in MILP; continuous draughts remain free. The LP is still a continuous relaxation. Optional CP uses the same physical integrality rather than allowing fractional grid steps. In `tank_reference_mode='pressed'`, both the assembler and its GM predictor use full-capacity tank arms and zero **adjustable** free-surface reserve, while retaining fixed-liquid FSM. The default half-fill/continuous lane is unchanged. This discrete lane may miss feasible slack-tank recipes, so no local infeasibility is a map certificate. The arrival builder separately removes reference slack-tank FSM/centroid artifacts from its native calibration; it must not double-credit the original free-surface offset.

Regressions added for anchors, native CB, full-tank arms/fixed FSM retention and physical tank integrality. These latest code changes were AST-checked only while two parent development jobs occupied both numerical slots; they require numerical verification before freezing.

Joint LP screening: every uncovered variable is now fixed to zero in the joint relaxation, matching the complete integer tail. If this exact local root relaxation returns explicit solver status 2 before any dive fixing, the method records `local_joint_lp_infeasible` and skips the integer tail. A timed-out/iteration-limited/unknown root result does not trigger this shortcut. The statement concerns this bounded local affine model only, never the whole physical map. Two requested regressions passed (PID 12476, exit 0; `results/lp-root-skip-tests.log`): no tail call follows a complete root-LP infeasibility, and the joint LP cannot evade a cargo mass cap by paying for an uncovered box. Nonjoint behavior is unchanged. A third regression for unknown-root fallback was added for the next full suite.

The final permitted joint integer-tail attempt now releases every greedy dive fixing and retains only the immutable arrival pairs. Earlier bounded attempts still release one fixing batch at a time. This spends no additional attempt and gives the full free-cargo assignment one chance within the declared count; it avoids stopping after four tails while five greedy batches still constrain the result. Nonjoint behavior is unchanged. ## Primary tail choice after the medium performance diagnostic

The primary method selects `joint_tail_solver='cp'` and `pairs_per_box=64` through its recorded method/profile settings. `Settings` keeps its experimental default MILP lane for direct comparisons; the method explicitly chooses CP. A fixed arrival box always retains its sole original edge. Every free box keeps at most 64 statically eligible edges, selected with the recorded deterministic seed. At 168 movable boxes this gives at most 10,752 free assignment edges, rather than approximately 37,800 in the unrestricted medium development model. Sampling may remove every feasible integer assignment even when the unsampled model has one; no sampling failure is an infeasibility proof.

CP-SAT uses one worker, the profile's deterministic-time and conflict limits, and a fixed number of tail attempts. Dive iterations, LP calls and LP iteration counts remain finite and recorded. The last permitted joint tail releases all greedy fixings while retaining actual arrival prefixes. These are limits on declared search work, not wall-clock deadlines; an ongoing native physics/checker call remains atomic. No wall time is used to decide which constructor candidate is returned.

Physical auxiliary variables use the declared tank-fraction grid (1e-4) and aft/forward draught grid (1e-3 m). Pressed tanks additionally enforce physical fraction integrality 0/1. The builder automatically requests `equality_grid_reserve=True` for the CP tail: each affine buoyancy and longitudinal equilibrium row receives an explicit construction band equal to one full auxiliary grid step in that row's original kg or kg·m units. This band permits a nearby continuous tangent equilibrium to survive finite-grid conversion. It does not change mass, draught, GM, trim, list, TCG or checker limits. Conservative integer coefficient/RHS rounding and finite grids can still miss feasible continuous recipes. All candidate cargo actions and decoded water masses are replayed and evaluated by the ordinary full checker; only its unpeeled pass counts as T5. The CP model and its equilibrium band cannot certify a physical pass or map infeasibility.

Optional local finishing directions: `ballast_opt.optimize(preferred_groups=None)` retains its original DB-F/DB-M/FWD-DEEP/HEEL default. The method's explicit finishing configuration now includes WING-A alongside those groups, so the native local LP can change a slack aft-wing pair whose finite-angle liquid transfer hurts righting area. Inputs must be a nonempty finite list/tuple of tank-group names; missing vessel groups are skipped and actual selected directions are recorded in `group_restriction`. The evaluation/iteration/LP limits do not increase. Adding a direction gives no guarantee: the local Jacobian, trust region and held pressed/slack regime may still miss a favorable empty/full corner, and full checking remains the acceptance gate. Synthetic direction-selection and count-limit regressions were added; they await the parent's integrated suite.
