## Recording notes

- Replans take about five minutes, so use prepared sentences with stored plans, labelled as recorded. The reading itself runs live.
- Shot 3c is typed, with Wi-Fi really off on camera, and uses the published adapter. Never say "offline" over the spoken path: speech to text is a cloud call.
- "One port per hatch" is never shown as an instruction. It's a preference that starts search from port-blocked plans.
- Don't show a bay closure as a hero or as "a crane is down". On these sections every closure is impossible, which comes from the section size.
- Shot 8's card holds still long enough to read. The limits sit on the same card as the percentage, not in a footnote.
- Say "search software", never "StowLM", over a plan the search made. Say "StowLM Reader" for the reading.
- Close notifications, hide the terminal. No company or vessel names on screen.
