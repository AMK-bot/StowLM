These are stock Google Translate public TTS synthetic voices, not a recording or a real-person voice. The radio channel is a disclosed simulation, not real radio: the existing `voice.audio.radio` applies a first-order 300 Hz high-pass followed by 3400 Hz low-pass, then seeded uniform white noise at 12 dB whole-clip RMS SNR after filtering, with peak scaling to avoid clipping.
