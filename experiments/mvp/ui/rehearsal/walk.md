## Findings not fixed

- **A. The live reader can't read the hero sentence on the hero ship in a v6 launch.** `--reader glimmer` serves only the brief panel (`/api/brief/*`), whose ships are the six brief-registry slices; the v6 hero ship isn't among them. On `sim.small.40100002` and `random.small.40100003.0.2` the live reader refused the hero sentence as `unsupported` (4.6 s cold, 2.1–2.2 s warm); it read the prepared "Bay 01 is closed…" live to "Nothing loads into bay 01." in 1.64 s. The v6 reading chapter always shows the recorded reading (labelled "Recorded answer"). Wiring the hero ship into the brief registry is a feature, not a rehearsal fix.
- **B. The brief panel (key V) in a v6 launch sits under the chapter overlay** on full-frame chapters (its buttons are covered), and on ship chapters it shows prepared chips stuck at "CHECKING" because v6 skips the brief gates (`app/brief.py` `start_gates`). Don't press V during a v6 demo.
- **C.** `favicon.ico` 404 in the console (benign).
- **D.** `app/tests/test_stage_director.py::test_v6_captions_are_verbatim_and_every_segment_of_the_script_reaches_its_shot` fails against `experiments/mvp/video/script-v6.md` as edited at 14:42 (a caption about "bay 9 garbled"); not touched by this rehearsal.
