# KCS-offset production linear-model study

**Status: the current production nine-case study is incomplete.** The first batch finished with all nine outcomes dirty or failed: external checker source and fleet inputs changed during measurement. Four complete 192+128 measurements are retained with `source_clean=false`; they are historical evidence only. Later cases rejected mixed hull/tank inputs or failed the physical sampling coverage requirement. No current production accuracy table or checker-approved departure is claimed. Historical analytic-hull results in the parent directory also do not validate the revised fleet.

`batch-status.json`, individual logs, source/input snapshots and `compute-timing.json` preserve this attempt. The study generator and explicit protocols below are ready for a fresh, source/input-stable run. The under-deck HC eligibility variant is implemented but has not yet been piloted on the revised fleet.

The synthetic feeder hulls use the published KCS body plan, scaled to declared dimensions. The first-attempt inputs had 440/840/1432 cells; a later read-only snapshot already had 372/592/1604 and revised tank IDs. Consequently all dimensions, capacities and timings below refer to their recorded epoch, not a promise about the current mutable fleet. The raw file is `data/raw/kcs/kcs.fix`, SHA-256 `aafc7e4f4d773bc50452d397985554c6a20f054150b8865ef70d61f836809190`. Fleet lightship, cargo capacities and D22 strength envelopes remain synthetic assumptions, rather than surveyed commercial-vessel approval data.

Light, medium and full mean 20%, 60% and 100% of the smaller of aggregate stack mass limits and design displacement less declared fixed mass and half-full ballast. The superseded first-attempt ships were stack-limited: 3,520,000 / 8,960,000 / 14,740,000 kg for feeders 500 / 1000 / 1700. Every draw preserves its case's fixed manifest, real 20 ft and known 40 ft HC dimensions, gross masses, physical support and stack limits. Light/medium use approximately 30% HC physical TEU tokens. **The feeder 1000 medium case additionally requires its known HC boxes under deck**; this is an explicit fixture eligibility assumption, not a general result for freely stowable HC cargo. The uniform full-mass protocol instead uses standard 20 ft boxes only, with 30.48 t boxes at the bottom and a lighter remainder box on top of each saturated stack. Full cargo mass does not imply all TEU positions occupied. The protocol deliberately minimises cargo KG within this declared box type/mass limit; comparisons across the ladder therefore also change box mix and vertical loading. These cases contain no OOG cargo.

Light/medium bands restrict absolute trim to 0.4 m and retain full adjustable ballast ranges and operational list limits. Full cases retain the declared operational trim band because their saturated stack masses fix cargo LCG. Full manifests use maximum-gross bottom boxes plus a lighter top remainder to attain each stack mass limit, rather than filling every tier with light cargo. There is no inherited special 1000-ship near-full-ballast restriction. Any subsequent necessary band change will be explicit. Calibration is 192 conditions (seeds 3701/3702), with 128 separate held-out conditions (seed 3712). Point-estimate GM uses known-fill free surfaces; hard LP constraints separately reserve maximum slack-fill FSM. Errors measure the point estimate, not that deliberate extra reserve.

Each final case records source/component versions and fleet-parameter/raw-offset hashes before and after measurement, production cell/station counts, strength-envelope SHA-256, and every station's SF/BM maximum and p99. One accepted held-out sample per ship is compared with the public stability and strength functions using physical masses and true box centres as equivalent fixed point loads. That comparison covers GM/trim/list/SF/BM, not cargo GZ, weather, full plan legality or approval.

The sampling proposal is physical but empirical and incomplete: its greedy placement and restricted 40 ft pairs do not enumerate every possible layout. Measured maxima are evidence over sampled conditions, not uniform mathematical bounds. Any held-out counterexample exceeding calibration remains visible; consumers require margins beyond measured error and an exact checker gate.

Initial one-calibration/four-held-out pilot (`pilot.json`): feeder 500 light, 4/4 accepted, GM maximum 0.0446026 m. Context 42.13 s, cold build 66.98 s, held-out sweep 5.06 s, total 114.17 s on one thread. This pilot predates the final exact KM-support acceleration and is not the completed production study.

The broader 1700 medium pilot exposed a genuine 0.170805 m GM error at trim about 1.466 m. Its even-keel KM interpolation error was below 0.00019 m; free-trim KM changed by 0.1847 m. This motivates the stated 0.4 m trim restriction rather than hiding the failure by narrowing displacement alone. An adaptive physical-placement pilot within that band accepted 4/6 attempts, with maximum GM error 0.0347714 m, build 3.42 s and held-out sweep 6.17 s. These small pilots are not substitutes for the final 320-condition measurements.

The original unrestricted-HC feeder 1000 medium sampler failed calibration after 3,200 attempts: only 31 of the required 64 conditions were accepted, with no hydrostatic LP projection for many high-KG layouts. Its original log is preserved. This is an open proposal-coverage limitation, not a proof that the free-HC case is impossible. The under-deck eligibility variant changes that case’s exact manifest identity and is reported separately; it does not justify a universal accuracy claim.

The first batch took 17.903 min from first admitted lease to termination, after 14.192 min queued at zero workers (32.095 min controller total). Budget changed 1→5→2 as host load rose; summed child process wall was 63.907 min, which is not CPU time. The 35 min successful-study estimate cannot be assessed against this failed attempt. Final full-case mixed-HC pilots exceeded the aspirational GM target (about 0.13/0.23 m for 500/1700), exposing the even-keel KM model’s free-trim limitation; those counterexamples remain in the pilot artifacts.
